1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mahadev Infoway ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the ToolKit mobile application (the "App"), available on Android and iOS.

ToolKit is designed as an offline-first utility app. Almost everything you do inside the App — compressing an image, turning photos into a PDF, reading text from a photo, calculating an EMI, generating a QR code, writing a note — happens entirely on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a server that stores your personal files, notes, photos, or calculations.

This policy describes the limited data that is collected — through device permissions you grant, through anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the App, and through third-party services used for advertising and payments — and explains your choices regarding that data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

ToolKit does not require registration, sign-in, or any personal profile. You may optionally type or choose content in certain tools — for example, notes in the Notepad, numbers in a calculator, countdown events in the Date Calculator, options on the Random Picker wheel, or text in Text Tools. Anything the App keeps is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us. Some content is not kept at all: text you type in Text Tools, and photos and text in the Text Extractor, are discarded when you leave the screen.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's tools and features.
  • To read text from photos in the Text Extractor using Google ML Kit, entirely on your device. Your photos and the recognised text are never uploaded.
  • To fetch currency exchange rates from the free Frankfurter API (sourced from the European Central Bank) for the Unit Converter's currency category. No personal data is sent with this request — only the currency codes needed for the conversion.
  • To understand, through anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), which tools and screens are used most, so we can fix problems and decide what to improve.
  • To adjust certain App settings remotely (Firebase Remote Config) — for example, how often interstitial ads are shown — without requiring an App update.
  • To display advertisements (free version) through Google AdMob, and to measure ad performance.
  • To process and validate ToolKit Pro subscription purchases and restore them across your devices.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse related to advertising or billing.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use the content of your notes, calculations, photos, PDFs, QR codes, or files for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than performing the function you requested within the App.

6. Advertising & Your Ad Choices

The free version of ToolKit shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the Home screen, occasional interstitial ads between actions, and native ads — styled like the App's cards — in the Home grid, on the Language screen, in Settings, and at the bottom of some tool screens. Native ads are always clearly labeled ("Ad").

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, ToolKit displays a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before loading any ads, in line with applicable regulations. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices in the App. Google Analytics for Firebase also respects this consent choice.

You can generally opt out of personalized advertising, or reset or delete your advertising identifier, from your device's own privacy settings (for example, Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy > Tracking on iOS).

Purchasing ToolKit Pro removes all ads (banner, interstitial, and native) from the App for as long as your subscription is active.

7. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

ToolKit offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, ToolKit Pro (currently a weekly plan, occasionally offered with a free trial), which unlocks additional features listed in the App and removes ads.

  • All payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store — ToolKit never receives or stores your card or payment details.
  • Your subscription status is checked with the store at every app launch to keep your entitlement accurate and up to date.
  • You can cancel or manage your subscription at any time from your Google Play or App Store account settings.
  • The "Restore Purchases" option in the App lets you reapply a previous purchase if you reinstall the App or switch devices.

8. Data Storage & Security

Almost all data created or used within ToolKit — notes, calculator history, saved World Clock cities, Date Calculator countdowns, Random Picker options, converter rates and preferences, language and theme choices, and locked-note PINs (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text) — is stored locally on your device using standard local storage mechanisms. Files you create — compressed photos, PDFs, QR code images, and ZIP files — are saved on your device or shared only where you choose to share them. We do not operate cloud servers that back up, sync, or store this content.

Because this data lives on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage will permanently delete it. We recommend copying anything important elsewhere before doing so.

We take reasonable technical measures appropriate to a mobile app to protect the limited data that does pass through third-party services (such as usage statistics and purchase records), but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data may be shared only as follows:

  • With Google AdMob, to the extent necessary to serve and measure ads (see Section 6).
  • With Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config), limited to the anonymous usage statistics and technical data described in Section 2.2.
  • With Google ML Kit, limited to performance and usage metrics of the text-recognition feature — never your photos or the recognised text.
  • With Google Play / Apple, to the extent necessary to process subscription purchases.
  • With the Frankfurter API, limited to the currency codes needed to fetch an exchange rate — no personal identifiers are included in this request.
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Mahadev Infoway, our users, or others, as permitted by law.

10. Your Rights & Choices

  • Permissions: Grant or revoke any App permission at any time via your device Settings.
  • Ad personalization and consent: Change your consent from Settings > Ad privacy choices in the App (where shown), opt out via your device's ad settings, or remove ads entirely with ToolKit Pro.
  • Usage statistics: Resetting or deleting your advertising ID on your device limits how usage statistics are linked over time. You may also contact us to request deletion of the usage data associated with your App installation.
  • Local data deletion: Clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored content.
  • Subscription management: Cancel or modify your ToolKit Pro subscription directly through Google Play or the App Store.
  • Access/correction requests: Since we do not hold personal profiles or cloud-stored personal content on our servers, most such requests are resolved directly on your device. For anything related to usage statistics or purchase records, contact us using the details in Section 15.

Depending on your location (for example, under GDPR in the EU/UK, or CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have additional rights regarding any personal data processed by our third-party service providers. Please refer to their respective privacy policies (Section 5) or contact us for guidance.

11. Children's Privacy

ToolKit is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

12. Data Retention

Locally stored content (notes, history, countdowns, preferences) remains on your device for as long as the App is installed, or until you delete it yourself. Usage statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are kept for a limited period (no longer than 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Data processed by other third-party services (Google AdMob, Google ML Kit, Google Play Billing) is retained according to their own retention policies, which are outside our control.

13. International Users

ToolKit is available globally and in multiple languages. If you access the App from outside your home country, be aware that limited data processed by third-party services described in this policy (such as AdMob, Firebase, or billing providers) may be transferred to and processed in other countries, including the United States, in accordance with those providers' own safeguards and privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or our practices. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. Significant changes may also be highlighted within the App. Continued use of ToolKit after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

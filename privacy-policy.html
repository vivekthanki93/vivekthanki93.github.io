Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app GeoStamp: GPS Map Camera (package com.mahadevinfoway.geostamp, the "App") handles information. The App is published by Mahadev Infoway ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short
  • There is no account or sign-in, and we run no server that receives your photos.
  • Your photos, saved places and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • To build the stamp, your location is sent to map, address and weather services.
  • The free version shows ads (Google AdMob), and we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used.

1. Information the App uses

Camera and photos

The App uses your camera to take photos and adds a stamp to them (address, map, coordinates, date and time, and other details you choose). Photos are saved in the App's own storage and in your phone's gallery. They also carry location and date details inside the file (EXIF data). We never upload your photos. They leave your phone only when you choose to share or email them, or create a report.

Location

The App reads your precise location only while the camera screen is open (foreground only, never in the background). It uses your location to:

  • show coordinates, altitude and accuracy on the stamp;
  • look up the address and draw a map of the spot;
  • get the current weather, if you turn on a weather field;
  • check whether you are within the allowed range of a saved place, and detect fake (mock) locations.

Saved places you create (name, address and coordinates) are stored only on your phone.

Microphone (optional)

Only if you turn on the "Sound level" field, the App uses the microphone to measure the noise level in decibels. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere; only the number is shown on the stamp.

QR code detection (optional)

If you turn on QR code detection, camera frames are read on your device using Google ML Kit. The images are not stored or sent to us.

Your own details

Text you type for the stamp or reports (note, name, contact number, company, project and similar) and logos you pick stay on your phone. They appear on your photos and reports, so they are shared with whoever you share those with.

Email after capture

If you choose to email photos, the App opens your own email app with the photo attached and the addresses you set. The email is sent by your email app, not by us.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play (or the App Store). Payment is handled entirely by them; we never see your card or payment details. The App only checks with the store which plan you own.

Usage and device information

Through Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob, the services listed below may collect information such as your device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, advertising ID, app instance ID, and how the App is used (for example, screens opened, photos taken, settings changed, and ads shown or clicked). Analytics does not receive your photos, coordinates or addresses.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

  • On your phone: photos, saved places, settings and report details stay until you delete them. Deleting a photo in the App also deletes its gallery copy where the phone allows it. Uninstalling the App removes its own data; photos already in your gallery stay until you delete them there.
  • Analytics: Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months, then deletes it automatically.
  • Ads: AdMob data is kept under Google's own retention rules.
  • You can ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance by emailing us (see Contact). Because there is no account, we may need the App's instance ID, which we can explain how to find.

6. Security

Data sent to the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Settings on your phone are kept in the system's secure storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Since your photos and details stay on your phone, you control most of it directly. For anything else, contact us.
